No. 03 — The machine

Tour-level sim.Bar-level vibe.

Full Swing is the simulator the pros keep at home. Dual-tracking cameras read the ball and the club, so a fade is a fade and a chunk is a chunk. It plays famous courses, runs a range with real numbers, and hosts games your non-golfer friends will actually enjoy.
